# Resources — Chapter 8: Finding the work

Everything referenced in [the chapter](../README.md), grouped by how central it is, with a short
note on what each one is for. Sources reviewed for the October 2026 revision. Hiring surveys,
job-posting analyses and compensation submissions cover different populations, and none predicts an
individual outcome. Check current postings and local pay before acting on an old figure.

## Start here

### [How to Interview and Hire ML/AI Engineers](https://eugeneyan.com/writing/how-to-interview/)

_Eugene Yan and Jason Liu_ &nbsp;·&nbsp; Practitioner account &nbsp;·&nbsp; Free &nbsp;·&nbsp; under
an hour

A hiring-side rubric for turning project decisions into interview evidence. See
[Chapter 1's annotation](../../01-getting-oriented/resources/README.md). Confirm each employer's
actual format with the recruiter.

### [AI Engineer Compensation Trends (Q3 2025)](https://www.levels.fyi/blog/ai-engineer-compensation-trends-q3-2025.html)

_Alina Kolesnikova, levels.fyi_ &nbsp;·&nbsp; Historical analysis &nbsp;·&nbsp; Free &nbsp;·&nbsp;
under an hour

Useful for seeing how one pooled AI premium can hide differences by level. The site's 2025 sample
reports a 6.2% AI/non-AI difference at entry level and 18.7% at staff level. These are historical
patterns in submitted pay, not a causal return from studying AI or a current salary target. The
article is now over a year old, so pair it with current local postings and the live tracker below.
Self-selection and the employer and region mix are limitations.

### [normcore-llm-reads](https://gist.github.com/veekaybee/be375ab33085102f9027853128dc5f0e)

_Vicki Boykis_ &nbsp;·&nbsp; Curated reading list &nbsp;·&nbsp; Free &nbsp;·&nbsp; varies

A named engineer's reading list can help narrow a noisy feed. Pick one item that answers a question
in your project, follow its evidence, and stop when you have the answer. A curated list is a
starting point, and some links may have aged. Boykis's
[essay on building AI applications](https://vickiboykis.com/2023/07/18/what-we-dont-talk-about-when-we-talk-about-building-ai-apps/)
gives useful practitioner context on operating details that demos often omit.

### [State of AI Report 2026](https://www.stateof.ai/)

_Nathan Benaich and Air Street Capital_ &nbsp;·&nbsp; Annual industry report &nbsp;·&nbsp; Free
&nbsp;·&nbsp; selected sections

The current edition covers research, industry, policy and safety. Use it to find a research
direction or sector, then read the linked original paper or company disclosure and check real
openings. It is investor-written analysis with investment interests, not an independent hiring
study. A benchmark gain, funding announcement or revenue forecast does not show demand for junior
staff. The site root rolls forward, so record the edition and slide number when citing a finding.

## Optional depth

### [AI Exposure Isn't Squeezing Advertised Pay in the US — It's Boosting It](https://hiringlab.indeed.com/2026/09/17/ai-exposure-isnt-squeezing-advertised-pay-in-the-us-its-boosting-it/)

_Jack Kennedy, Indeed Hiring Lab_ &nbsp;·&nbsp; Original job-posting analysis &nbsp;·&nbsp; Free
&nbsp;·&nbsp; under an hour

A September 2026 analysis of US postings that advertise an **annual salary**. Occupations more
exposed to AI show faster advertised-pay growth, but occupation and seniority mix affect the
comparison. The seniority-specific pattern is suggestive, not conclusive. Read the methodology
before interpreting a headline. Salary-advertising postings skew more senior than all postings,
exposure is scored per occupation, and advertised pay is not realised pay. It does not isolate AI's
causal effect or show entry-level pay in another country.

### [CIRR verified bootcamp outcomes](https://www.cirr.org/schooldata)

_Council on Integrity in Results Reporting_ &nbsp;·&nbsp; Outcome reports &nbsp;·&nbsp; Free
&nbsp;·&nbsp; under an hour

Use it to check a specific programme and cohort before you pay for training. Find the complete
report and check its dates, graduation denominator, employment definition, follow-up window and
audit status. An old cohort or a placeholder rate cannot back a current placement promise.
Software-engineering bootcamp outcomes do not directly predict an AI/ML self-study search. A missing
current report is missing evidence, not proof that every provider fabricates outcomes. One school's
headline cannot tell you how long every search takes.

### [PwC 2026 Global AI Jobs Barometer](https://www.pwc.com/gx/en/issues/artificial-intelligence/job-barometer/2026/2026-global-ai-jobs-barometer-full-report.pdf)

_PwC research team_ &nbsp;·&nbsp; Original job-ad analysis &nbsp;·&nbsp; Free &nbsp;·&nbsp; selected
sections

The report and its
[official release](https://www.pwc.com/gx/en/news-room/press-releases/2026/pwc-2026-ai-jobs-barometer.html)
analyse over a billion job adverts across 27 countries/territories and separately examine US
entry-level skill requirements. Use the definitions, period and comparison groups to see how
advertised skills are changing. Its reported 62% average AI-skill wage premium is not a beginner's
expected raise. Observational patterns cannot show that AI caused hiring or productivity changes.
PwC sells AI advisory services too. Build a local role-requirement worksheet instead of copying a
global premium into your salary target.

### [Machine Learning Interview Prep Guide](https://www.aced.io/blog/machine-learning-interview-guide)

_Aced, formerly Exponent_ &nbsp;·&nbsp; Commercial guide &nbsp;·&nbsp; Free article &nbsp;·&nbsp;
selected sections

An optional checklist of interview topics and formats. Compare it with the hiring-side account above
and the employer's instructions. Proprietary preparation frameworks are not industry standards. The
company sells interview preparation, so separate the free topic map from its product
recommendations. Tool permissions, round counts and expectations vary by employer and can change
faster than a year-labelled article.

## Keep for reference

### [AI Engineer Roadmap](https://roadmap.sh/ai-engineer)

_roadmap.sh maintainers_ &nbsp;·&nbsp; Topic map &nbsp;·&nbsp; Free &nbsp;·&nbsp; lookup

A vocabulary and coverage cross-check, especially for application work with pretrained models. Its
[AI and Data Scientist map](https://roadmap.sh/ai-data-scientist) has a different scope. Use either
to look up an unfamiliar term, then return to the chapter sequence and your project. A topic graph
cannot tell you the time, prerequisite depth or evidence your role needs. Nodes differ in value, so
check each linked resource's current version.

### [ML / AI Software Engineer salary tracker](https://www.levels.fyi/t/software-engineer/focus/ml-ai)

_levels.fyi_ &nbsp;·&nbsp; Submitted compensation tracker &nbsp;·&nbsp; Free lookup &nbsp;·&nbsp;
varies

Filter by location, level, employer and recent date. Separate base salary, bonus and equity, because
total pay can move with stock valuation. Record the sample and filters with any comparison. US
aggregate medians do not describe an entry-level candidate in another region. Submitted data are
self-selected and may under-represent smaller employers and lower-paid offers. Use several current
local sources when negotiating.

### [ML-YouTube-Courses](https://github.com/dair-ai/ML-YouTube-Courses)

_DAIR.AI maintainers_ &nbsp;·&nbsp; Course index &nbsp;·&nbsp; Free &nbsp;·&nbsp; lookup

A handy way to find university lecture series. Before committing to a playlist, check the actual
course site, year, assignments and software versions. Older fundamentals can still be useful, though
an old framework walkthrough may need adapting. The index is a collection, not an ordered
curriculum, and repository popularity does not prove learning outcomes. Use one course to fill a
specific gap instead of adding several full courses.

### [Open Source Society University — Computer Science](https://github.com/ossu/computer-science)

_OSSU contributors_ &nbsp;·&nbsp; Broad self-study curriculum &nbsp;·&nbsp; Free/varies by provider
&nbsp;·&nbsp; long-term

A route to broader computing foundations for anyone who needs them. An experienced developer can
pick one gap such as algorithms or systems instead of starting a full degree-sized curriculum. The
materials do not lead to an accredited degree, and finishing coursework does not guarantee a job.
Course availability, optional certificates and time needed depend on the providers.
