# Resources — Chapter 8: Finding the work

Everything referenced in [the chapter](../README.md), grouped by how central it is, with a short
note on what each one is for. Sources reviewed for the October 2026 revision. Hiring surveys,
job-posting analyses and compensation submissions cover different populations; none predicts an
individual outcome. Check current postings and local compensation before acting on an old figure.

## Start here

### [How to Interview and Hire ML/AI Engineers](https://eugeneyan.com/writing/how-to-interview/)

_Eugene Yan and Jason Liu_ &nbsp;·&nbsp; Practitioner account &nbsp;·&nbsp; Free &nbsp;·&nbsp; under
an hour

A hiring-side rubric for turning project decisions into interview evidence. See
[Chapter 1's annotation](../../01-getting-oriented/resources/README.md); confirm your employer's
actual format with the recruiter.

### [AI Engineer Compensation Trends (Q3 2025)](https://www.levels.fyi/blog/ai-engineer-compensation-trends-q3-2025.html)

_Alina Kolesnikova, levels.fyi_ &nbsp;·&nbsp; Historical analysis &nbsp;·&nbsp; Free &nbsp;·&nbsp;
under an hour

Useful for seeing why a pooled AI premium can conceal differences by level. The site's 2025 sample
reports a 6.2% AI/non-AI difference at entry level and 18.7% at staff level. Those are historical
associations in submitted compensation, not a causal return to studying AI or a current salary
target. The article is now over a year old; pair it with current local postings and the live tracker
below. Treat self-selection and employer/region mix as limitations.

### [normcore-llm-reads](https://gist.github.com/veekaybee/be375ab33085102f9027853128dc5f0e)

_Vicki Boykis_ &nbsp;·&nbsp; Curated reading list &nbsp;·&nbsp; Free &nbsp;·&nbsp; varies

A named engineer's selection of technical reading can help narrow a noisy feed. Pick one item that
answers a question in your project, follow its evidence and stop when you have the answer. A curated
list is a starting point, not a guarantee that every link remains current. Boykis's
[essay on building AI applications](https://vickiboykis.com/2023/07/18/what-we-dont-talk-about-when-we-talk-about-building-ai-apps/)
is useful practitioner context on operating details that demos often omit.

### [State of AI Report 2026](https://www.stateof.ai/)

_Nathan Benaich and Air Street Capital_ &nbsp;·&nbsp; Annual industry report &nbsp;·&nbsp; Free
&nbsp;·&nbsp; selected sections

The current edition covers research, industry, policy and safety. Use it to discover a research
direction or sector, then read the linked original paper/company disclosure and check actual
openings. It is investor-authored analysis with investment interests, not an independently
controlled hiring study. A benchmark improvement, funding announcement or revenue forecast does not
establish demand for junior employees. The site root rolls forward: record the edition and slide
number whenever citing a specific finding.

## Optional depth

### [AI Exposure Isn't Squeezing Advertised Pay in the US — It's Boosting It](https://hiringlab.indeed.com/2026/09/17/ai-exposure-isnt-squeezing-advertised-pay-in-the-us-its-boosting-it/)

_Jack Kennedy, Indeed Hiring Lab_ &nbsp;·&nbsp; Original job-posting analysis &nbsp;·&nbsp; Free
&nbsp;·&nbsp; under an hour

A September 2026 analysis of US postings that advertise an **annual salary**. Higher AI-exposure
occupations have faster advertised-pay growth, but occupational and seniority mix affect the
comparison; the seniority-specific pattern is suggestive rather than conclusive. Read the
methodology before interpreting a headline. The salary-posting subset is more senior than all
postings, exposure is an occupation-level score, and advertised pay is not realized compensation. It
does not isolate AI's causal effect or establish entry-level pay in another country.

### [CIRR verified bootcamp outcomes](https://www.cirr.org/schooldata)

_Council on Integrity in Results Reporting_ &nbsp;·&nbsp; Outcome reports &nbsp;·&nbsp; Free
&nbsp;·&nbsp; under an hour

Use to investigate a specific program and cohort before paying for training. Find the complete
report and check its dates, graduation denominator, employment definition, follow-up window and
audit status. An old cohort or a placeholder rate cannot support a current placement promise.
Reported software-engineering bootcamp outcomes do not directly forecast an AI/ML self-study search.
Absence of a current report is missing evidence, not proof that all providers fabricate outcomes. Do
not infer a universal search duration from one school's headline.

### [PwC 2026 Global AI Jobs Barometer](https://www.pwc.com/gx/en/issues/artificial-intelligence/job-barometer/2026/2026-global-ai-jobs-barometer-full-report.pdf)

_PwC research team_ &nbsp;·&nbsp; Original job-ad analysis &nbsp;·&nbsp; Free &nbsp;·&nbsp; selected
sections

The report and its
[official release](https://www.pwc.com/gx/en/news-room/press-releases/2026/pwc-2026-ai-jobs-barometer.html)
analyze over a billion advertisements across 27 countries/territories and separately examine US
entry-level skill requirements. Use the definitions, period and comparison groups to understand how
advertised skills are changing. Its reported 62% average AI-skill wage premium is not an expected
raise for a beginner, and observational associations do not establish that AI caused hiring or
productivity changes. PwC also sells AI advisory services. Build a local role-requirement worksheet
rather than copying a global premium into your salary target.

### [Machine Learning Interview Prep Guide](https://www.aced.io/blog/machine-learning-interview-guide)

_Aced, formerly Exponent_ &nbsp;·&nbsp; Commercial guide &nbsp;·&nbsp; Free article &nbsp;·&nbsp;
selected sections

An optional checklist of interview topics and formats. Compare it with the hiring-side account above
and the employer's instructions; proprietary preparation frameworks are not industry standards. The
company sells interview preparation, so separate the free topic map from its product
recommendations. Tool permissions, round counts and expectations vary by employer and can change
faster than a year-labelled article.

## Keep for reference

### [AI Engineer Roadmap](https://roadmap.sh/ai-engineer)

_roadmap.sh maintainers_ &nbsp;·&nbsp; Topic map &nbsp;·&nbsp; Free &nbsp;·&nbsp; lookup

A vocabulary and coverage cross-check, especially for application work using pretrained models. Its
[AI and Data Scientist map](https://roadmap.sh/ai-data-scientist) serves a different scope. Use
either to identify an unfamiliar term, then return to the chapter sequence and your project. A topic
graph does not determine the time, prerequisite depth or evidence needed for your role. Check each
linked resource's current version rather than assuming all nodes have equal value.

### [ML / AI Software Engineer salary tracker](https://www.levels.fyi/t/software-engineer/focus/ml-ai)

_levels.fyi_ &nbsp;·&nbsp; Submitted compensation tracker &nbsp;·&nbsp; Free lookup &nbsp;·&nbsp;
varies

Filter by location, level, employer and recent date. Separate base salary, bonus and equity; total
compensation can fluctuate with stock valuation. Record the sample and filters with any comparison.
US aggregate medians do not describe an entry-level candidate in another region. Submitted data are
self-selected and may underrepresent smaller employers and lower-paid offers. Use multiple current
local sources when negotiating.

### [ML-YouTube-Courses](https://github.com/dair-ai/ML-YouTube-Courses)

_DAIR.AI maintainers_ &nbsp;·&nbsp; Course index &nbsp;·&nbsp; Free &nbsp;·&nbsp; lookup

A convenient way to locate university lecture series. Verify the actual course site, year,
assignments and software versions before committing to a playlist. Older fundamentals can still be
useful; an old framework walkthrough may need adaptation. The index is a collection, not a sequenced
curriculum, and repository popularity does not establish learning outcomes. Use one course to fill a
specific gap instead of adding several complete courses to the schedule.

### [Open Source Society University — Computer Science](https://github.com/ossu/computer-science)

_OSSU contributors_ &nbsp;·&nbsp; Broad self-study curriculum &nbsp;·&nbsp; Free/varies by provider
&nbsp;·&nbsp; long-term

A route to broader computing foundations for someone who needs them. For an experienced developer,
select a gap such as algorithms or systems rather than restarting a full degree-sized curriculum.
The materials do not award an accredited degree, and completing coursework does not guarantee a job.
Course availability, optional certificates and time requirements depend on the providers.
